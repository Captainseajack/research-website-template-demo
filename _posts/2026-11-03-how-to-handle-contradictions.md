Sometimes two sources appear to make different claims about the same event. Before treating them as irreconcilable, record the exact language, dates, definitions, and populations each source uses.

### What differs?

- Source B says: [insert claim and citation].
- My next step: [find original records, verify dates, or compare definitions].
