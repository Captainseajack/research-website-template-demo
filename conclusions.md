## The question

**What specific claim or policy question is this research attempting to answer?** Write it plainly, in a sentence or two.

## Executive summary

Put your answer here. Aim for 3–6 paragraphs that a reader can understand without opening every source. Explain your argument, what evidence most strongly supports it, and the most significant uncertainty.

## Key findings

### Finding 1 — State the conclusion clearly

Explain the finding in ordinary language. Link the underlying document or your source record wherever possible. For example, you can link [to a sample source record]({{ '/sources/example-official-record/' | relative_url }}).

### Finding 2 — Distinguish evidence from inference

Describe what the source directly establishes, then separately explain your interpretation of it. A claim supported by a primary document should not be presented as established merely because it appeared in a social-media post.

### Finding 3 — What would change this assessment?

State what additional information could cause you to revise a finding. This keeps the conclusions readable without pretending the investigation is finished.

## Limitations and open questions

- **Unresolved question:** What information is still missing?
- **Conflicting evidence:** Which sources appear to disagree, and why?
- **Limits of the record:** Does an important document lack context, or rely on an estimate?
